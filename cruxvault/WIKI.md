### comparison

- [[count-models-in-single-cell-genomics\|Count models in single-cell genomics]] — The single-cell field's decade-long argument about how to model raw counts — NB vs Poisson, zero-inflation, library-size offsets, and whether to transform at all.
- [[genomic-language-models\|Genomic language models]] — Large self-supervised models over raw DNA at megabase context — Evo 2 and Nucleotide Transformer v3 — and what they add beyond supervised sequence-to-function models.
- [[query-decoders-and-conditional-computation\|Query decoders and conditional computation]] — Generating or selecting decoder weights per query instead of sharing one kernel — sparsely-gated mixture-of-experts, CondConv, and dynamic convolution.
- [[sequence-conditioned-epigenome-models\|Sequence-conditioned epigenome models]] — Models that predict epigenomic and transcriptomic signal from DNA sequence (± chromatin accessibility) — Enformer, BPNet, EPCOT, dHICA, TFImpute, DNABERT — and how they differ from tensor-based imputation.
- [[sequence-model-critiques\|Critiques of sequence-to-function models]] — The evidence that DNA-sequence models fail on individual variation, ignore distal enhancers, and that DNA language-model embeddings underperform one-hot — while supervised functional pretraining does transfer.

### concept

- [[average-activity-baseline\|Average activity baseline]] — Predicting an assay's mean signal across training cell types at each locus — the naive baseline that outperformed all but two of 23 ENCODE Imputation Challenge submissions.
- [[chip-seq-assay-and-controls\|ChIP-seq assay design and controls]] — ENCODE/modENCODE working standards: antibody validation, replication, the mandatory input control, and why the control channel is the reference every enrichment measure is defined against.
- [[count-distributions-for-sequencing-data\|Count distributions for sequencing data]] — Poisson and negative binomial models of read counts, the overdispersion that forces the NB, and the variance-stabilising transforms used when counts are fed to a neural network.
- [[covariate-conditioning-and-counterfactuals\|Covariate conditioning and counterfactuals]] — Making a model respond to an explicit covariate rather than absorbing it into the latent — adversarial disentangling, compositional embeddings, and how to test that conditioning is actually used.
- [[cross-cell-type-generalization-pitfall\|Cross-cell-type generalisation pitfall]] — Sharing genomic loci between train and test lets a model score well by memorising per-locus average activity; and performance on well-characterised cell types does not predict performance on poorly characterised ones.
- [[distributional-shift-and-batch-effects\|Distributional shift and batch effects]] — Processing and protocol differences across a compendium shift the signal distribution site-specifically; the ENCODE challenge's deduplication artefact is the canonical worked example.
- [[imputation-evaluation-measures\|Imputation evaluation measures]] — Why MSE and global correlation are assay-dependent and mutually redundant, and the challenge's replacement measures partitioned by signal strength, cell-type specificity, promoters, and peaks.
- [[jepa-and-collapse-prevention\|JEPA and collapse prevention]] — Predicting in representation space invites collapse; LeJEPA's SIGReg, VICReg's variance floor, and effective rank as a label-free health metric are the current answers.
- [[masked-self-supervised-learning\|Masked self-supervised learning]] — Corrupt-and-reconstruct pre-training across text, images and tabular data — BERT, MAE, I-JEPA, VIME — and the design choices (mask ratio, prediction space) that decide what is learned.
- [[read-processing-and-artifact-regions\|Read processing and artefact regions]] — Alignment, filtering, deduplication and blacklisting — the pipeline steps between FASTQ and signal, and the ones that silently create distributional shifts.
- [[sequencing-depth-and-coverage\|Sequencing depth and coverage]] — How many reads an epigenomic experiment needs, why the answer differs by mark, and why depth is a first-class experimental covariate rather than a nuisance to be scaled away.
- [[set-conditioned-modelling-and-missingness\|Set-conditioned modelling and structured missingness]] — Attention over a variable-size observed set rather than a fixed tensor with holes — Set Transformer, Conditional Neural Processes, and why block-structured missingness breaks MCAR/MAR assumptions.
- [[signal-normalization-in-epigenomics\|Signal normalisation in epigenomics]] — The methods that try to make epigenomic tracks comparable across experiments — scaling, S3norm, spike-in, CHIPIN, quantile variants — and the assumption each one makes.
- [[training-mechanics\|Training mechanics]] — The optimiser and schedule choices that have derivations behind them — decoupled weight decay and cosine annealing with warm restarts — and automated search over the configurations they define.
- [[uncertainty-calibration\|Uncertainty and calibration]] — Modern networks are systematically overconfident; calibration is measurable, and in genomics the posterior probabilities emitted by SAGA models are a worked example of miscalibration with real consequences.

### dataset

- [[encode-imputation-challenge\|ENCODE Imputation Challenge (EIC)]] — The 2019–2023 prospective benchmark of 23 imputation methods: its two-stage design, the 35-assay × 51-biosample panel, and its finding that a naive baseline beat almost every entrant.
- [[reference-epigenome-compendia\|Reference epigenome compendia]] — ENCODE, Roadmap, IHEC and EpiMap — the consortium datasets that define the cell-type × assay matrix, its sparsity, and its metadata conventions.
- [[transcriptome-and-annotation-resources\|Transcriptome and annotation resources]] — GENCODE, GTEx, FANTOM5 and FANTOM6 — the gene models, expression references and promoter atlases used to define features and to validate epigenomic predictions biologically.

### method

- [[avocado\|Avocado]] — Schreiber et al. 2020: deep multi-scale tensor factorisation that both imputes epigenomic tracks and yields a reusable latent representation of the genome; extended to the full ENCODE3 compendium.
- [[chromatin-state-annotation\|Chromatin state annotation (SAGA)]] — ChromHMM and Segway summarise multi-assay epigenomic data into labelled genome segmentations; their annotations are a primary downstream consumer of imputed tracks, and are irreproducible more often than their posteriors suggest.
- [[chromimpute\|ChromImpute]] — Ernst & Kellis 2015: per-target ensembles of regression trees over correlated marks and samples; the first large-scale epigenome imputation, and the only one that denoises without retraining.
- [[edice\|eDICE]] — Hawkins-Hooker et al. 2023: attention over observed tracks for epigenomic imputation, extended by transfer learning to individual-specific (donor-level) prediction.
- [[epigenome-denoising\|Epigenome denoising]] — Coda and AtacWorks: learning a mapping from low-quality to high-quality chromatin signal, generalising to unseen cell types — the direct ancestors of denoising-by-model rather than by imputation.
- [[film-conditioning\|FiLM conditioning]] — Feature-wise Linear Modulation: conditioning a network by predicting per-channel scale and shift from side information, applied throughout the network rather than at the input.
- [[peak-calling-and-signal-tracks\|Peak calling and signal tracks]] — MACS/MACS2 and the local-Poisson model behind the −log10 p-value tracks that essentially all epigenome imputation methods consume as their target.
- [[predictd\|PREDICTD]] — Durham et al. 2018: parallel factor (PARAFAC) tensor decomposition of the cell type × assay × position tensor, imputing all missing experiments in one jointly trained model.
- [[quantile-normalization\|Quantile normalisation]] — Forcing samples to share an empirical distribution — the default cross-sample normaliser, its failure when class or batch composition differs, and the smooth and peak/background variants.
- [[transformers-and-positional-encoding\|Transformers and positional encoding]] — Self-attention as the backbone architecture, and RoPE as the rotation-matrix scheme that injects relative position directly into the attention product.

### overview

- [[epigenome-imputation\|Epigenome imputation]] — Predicting unperformed epigenomic experiments from the correlation structure of observed ones; the task definition, its history, and why imputed data is often used for denoising.
